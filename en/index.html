About

Profile

I am Katsutomo Izawa, a photographer working professionally since 2013. Based in Tokyo as K's Creative Works, my work centres on advertising photography in beauty and fashion.

On set, I value the accumulation of judgement — deciding what to keep within limited time and conditions. The aim is always to understand the client's intent and capture the subject in the form that communicates their appeal most strongly.
